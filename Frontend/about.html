ABOUT SMARTCHARGE

Powering the Future of Electric Mobility

SmartCharge is an EV charging platform designed to make electric vehicle charging simple, accessible and convenient. Our platform helps EV users discover charging stations, find suitable connectors and make their charging journey easier.

We aim to build a connected charging network where EV owners can easily find reliable charging points and charger owners can share their chargers with other EV users.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make EV charging more accessible and convenient for everyone. We want to support the growth of electric mobility by connecting EV drivers with charging infrastructure wherever they need it.

What We Do

  • Find nearby EV charging stations
  • Search chargers based on connector type
  • Help EV users plan their charging journey
  • Allow charger owners to share their chargers
  • Build a connected EV charging community
